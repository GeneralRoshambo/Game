How to Play

The Board

The battlefield is a 3x3 grid: three lanes (left, center, right), each three cells deep — your home row, a neutral middle row, and the enemy's home row (and vice versa for them). Creatures deploy into your home row and can move Left, Right, Advance, or Retreat — one step per turn.

Combat

A creature can fight whatever enemy creature is directly adjacent to it (one row up or down, same lane). Whoever has the higher Rank wins; the loser is destroyed. Equal Ranks destroy both.

Attacking the Player — Center Lane Only

The side lanes (left and right) are skirmish lanes: creatures there can only ever fight other creatures, never the enemy player directly. Only the center lane leads to a Shield — a creature that fully advances through the center lane with nothing left to fight lands a direct hit on the enemy's Shields. The 🧍/👹 markers above and below the board show whose end is whose.

Shields (Your Life)

Each player starts with 3 face-down Shields. A direct hit destroys one Shield (it's discarded, and if it has a simple triggered ability, that resolves as a "Shield Trigger"). Take a direct hit with zero Shields left and you immediately lose.

Mana

There is one mana pool. You gain +2 mana at the start of every turn, and unused mana always carries over — it's never lost. Creatures of a suit you already control get cheaper (up to -2) the more of that suit you have in play — specializing in a suit pays off.

Card Types

Creatures occupy a board slot and usually trigger an ability when summoned. Spells resolve immediately and go to the discard pile. Equipment attaches to one of your creatures, giving it +1 Rank permanently plus its own ability.

Turn Order

Gain mana → draw a card → play creatures/spells/equipment and move/attack with your creatures, in any order, as many times as your mana and movement allow → End Turn.

Deckbuilding

Decks are exactly 40 cards, max 4 copies of any one card. Use the Deck & Cards screen to adjust your deck between runs, and the Card Creator to design your own cards — pick a suit, a rank (which sets the mana cost), and up to 3 abilities from that suit's list.

Suit Identities

♥ Hearts — healing, protection, revival, support.
♣ Clubs — aggression, poison, board control.
♦ Diamonds — economy, tokens, buffing, card advantage.
♠ Spades — removal, control, sacrifice.
